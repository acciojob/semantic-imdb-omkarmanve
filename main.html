Movie Summary

3 Idiots is a Bollywood comedy-drama that follows three engineering students, Rancho, Farhan, and Raju, as they navigate college life, academic pressure, friendship, and family expectations. The film encourages students to pursue knowledge, follow their passions, and learn for understanding rather than simply chasing success.
